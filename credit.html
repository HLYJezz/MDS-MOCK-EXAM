About the site

  • It is unofficial. A student's study site, not published by the college and not checked by it. Answer keys can be wrong — there is a Report a problem link under every question.
  • Your scores stay on your device. Answers, marks and attempt history are kept in this browser and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears them.
  • Built plainly. Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework and no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Type. Set in Inter by Rasmus Andersson, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and served from this site rather than a font CDN.
  • Visit counting. GoatCounter counts page visits without cookies and without identifying anyone. It never sees an answer or a score.
  • The logo belongs to the College of Medicine, Rangsit University, and appears here only to say which cohort the site is for.
